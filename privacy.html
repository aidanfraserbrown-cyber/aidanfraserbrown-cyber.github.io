WatchLab privacy policy

WatchLab is a personal project used only by its author.

What it reads

With the author's permission, it reads the author's own Google Health data (sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, oxygen saturation, activity) and readings from the author's own watch.

Where the data goes

The data is stored only on the author's own computer and watch. It is not sold, shared, sent to any other server, or used for advertising. Nobody else's data is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored files can be deleted from the author's computer.
